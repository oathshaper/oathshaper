# Imported model provenance

Release rights review, 2026-11-03: the project owner reports that the supplied characters, weapons, tower and garden were generated on a paid Meshy plan in private mode. [Meshy Terms of Service §3.2](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use) assigns paid-plan output ownership to the customer to the extent permitted by law; its [distribution guidance](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/9992022-can-i-sell-meshy-models-marketplaces-stores-licensing) permits distribution and sale outside Meshy. This clears Meshy-origin permission to include these assets in the demo and source download. Incorporated provider rigs/animations follow §3.1's grant for service assets incorporated in customer output.

The owner report is the source for account/plan status. Exact generation inputs were not independently inspected; the standard input-rights condition remains applicable. No concrete conflicting third-party claim was identified. Private generation does not forbid the owner from releasing the models. Earlier technical notes below describe successive imports; they are not additional permission gates. See the root [third-party notices](../../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) for the other asset sources.

## Tessa and party weapons — 2026-10-28

`rigged/player_rigged.glb` now derives from the supplied `Meshy_AI_Emerald_Pathfinder_All_Animations.glb`: 72 provider joints, 10,258 triangles, original 8K albedo and 4K surface maps. Walk/Run retain the supplied motion with foot alignment/contact adjustments; Idle is locally authored. The static `player.glb` is the historical fallback, not the source for this new rig. The supplied sabre, buckler, staff and mace replace primitive weapon art through named sockets. See [manifest and source filenames](equipment/manifest.json), [weapon provenance](equipment/README.md) and [integration evidence](../../../docs/party-assets-20261028/README.md).

## Garden adversaries — 2026-10-22

The supplied Lantern Sentinel, Iron Sentinel and Crimson Corsair replace the garden's primitive enemies. Their final game derivatives use the original embedded texture payloads, bounded geometry and locally authored weighted rigs. Source filenames, hashes, triangle counts, animation scope and reproduction commands are recorded in [the adversary provenance](adversaries/README.md) and [manifest](adversaries/manifest.json). The original Meshy exports were static; the skeletons and clips are local derivatives.

### Player coat-weight correction — 2026-10-22

The player rig derivative was regenerated with the existing Blender 5.0.1 pipeline plus a bounded lower-coat weight repair for 278 vertices. It keeps the skirt anchored to hips/spine during practice arm motions. The original `player.glb` and its rights status remain unchanged. See [rigging notes](../../../docs/rigging.md).

## Ivo

`ivo.glb` is a byte-identical copy of the textured Meshy export supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-20, named `Meshy_AI_Skybound_Rogue_1020142546_texture.glb`. Both copies are 7,776,812 bytes with SHA-256 `0b981c4ddbe9b99a28e10b11edf3260dd75acbd43084b0c234982de66fbd33ed`. Its shape and centered bounds closely match the first Ivo export. The first, untextured export was named `Meshy_AI_The_Brassbound_Voyage_1020031535_generate.glb`, was 285,464 bytes with SHA-256 `d29c2528aed8063e38d6834d515a4a73c34a26f4f31857a542b0651e7df96ea6`, and remains recoverable from Git commit `f88e890`.

The current GLB contains one static mesh with 11,232 vertices and 15,801 triangles, UVs, normals, and one material. Three embedded JPEG maps provide base color, metallic/roughness, and normals; each is 2048 × 2048 pixels. This is a textured export, not a 4K texture or a rigged character. It contains no skin, skeleton, or animation. Godot's import option `gltf/embedded_image_handling=3` keeps the maps in the imported scene without creating duplicate JPEG files alongside the GLB. The character slot leaves its material intact because it has a base-color texture. The fallback blue color is used only for an untextured model.

The scene prefers `rigged/ivo_rigged.glb`, a local Blender 5.0.1 derivative of that original with a weighted 19-bone skin and skeletal `Idle` and `Talk` clips. It retains the original static export as the fallback if the rigged import is absent or invalid. Character-specific arm angles leave both hands visible in his relaxed standing pose. The clips add subtle breathing and a conversation gesture through bone motion, without facial animation. Blender front/rear and Talk views and a 1280 × 800 Godot dialogue capture were inspected; this remains a first-pass skin around the jacket and hands.

The mesh is about 0.95 model units tall. [The scene](../../scenes/demo.tscn) scales it by `2` and uses its original `+0.10 m` offset at Ivo's `1.05 m` root height. [Grounding](../../scripts/grounded_idle_character.gd) samples the actual floor and aligns the capsule and lowest mesh point at runtime. A soft contact shadow stays fixed to the floor while the rig animates. Ivo's collider, label, and conversation remain scene-owned. [The current dialogue capture](../../../docs/images/rig-ivo-talk-1280x800.png) shows the skeletal derivative in Lantern Quay.

The release rights review above applies to this model and its local rigged derivative. The generated hub surfaces have separate provenance in [the hub map](../../../docs/hub-map.md).

On a fresh checkout, build the host extension and run the Godot editor or `Godot --headless --path godot --import` before launching the game. Both Ivo `.glb.import` settings keep LOD generation disabled and embed texture maps in the imported scene. The other character imports are recorded below.

## Mara

`mara.glb` is a byte-identical copy of the owner's textured Meshy export supplied on 2026-10-20, named `Meshy_AI_Embercoat_Wayfarer_1020143630_texture.glb`. Both copies are 7,900,144 bytes with SHA-256 `dbac1f4f7b4136c7c2a903860498448b79ee4779d00712c783128aa02c2d0507`. Its orange coat, blue inner tunic, dark hair, and belt lantern match the previously supplied [Mara concept](../../../art/concepts/mara-meshy-reference.png).

The original GLB has one static mesh with 17,157 vertices, 15,178 triangles, UVs, normals, and one material. Its three embedded JPEG maps are 2048 × 2048 pixels and supply base color, metallic/roughness, and normals. There is no skin, skeleton, or animation. Its centered mesh is about 0.95 model units tall; [the scene](../../scenes/demo.tscn) scales it by `2`, with a local `+0.10 m` offset. [Grounding](../../scripts/grounded_idle_character.gd) aligns its feet and scene-owned capsule to the quay. The imported model replaces Mara's primitive body, while her scene-owned interaction, label, and lamp light remain. [Scene glue](../../scripts/demo.gd) positions that light at the textured belt lantern and hides the primitive globe while the model is present; the original globe and bobbing idle remain as a fallback if the GLB is absent.

The scene now prefers `rigged/mara_rigged.glb`, a local Blender 5.0.1 derivative built from that exact original by [`tools/rigging/rig_characters.py`](../../../tools/rigging/rig_characters.py). Its 19-bone skin covers all 17,154 exported vertices, with `Idle` and `Talk` animations. The clips bring Mara's angled arms down beside her coat and narrow her stance through bone rotations. A keyed 9 mm model-space hip lift cancels the small boot drop caused by the leg pose; Blender's evaluated Idle sole is within about 0.2 mm of the source floor level at demo scale. The original GLB was not modified. The derivative retains all three original embedded JPEG payloads byte for byte, though Blender reordered them in the GLB. The fallback to the original static mesh remains if the rigged import is missing or invalid. This is first-pass weighting: coat-panel gaps and a few detached trim flecks around the legs still need a character art pass.

Both `.glb.import` settings embed the supplied maps in the imported scene, avoiding duplicate JPEG files. The release rights review above applies to both GLBs.

## Lantern tower

`lantern_tower.glb` is a byte-identical copy of the project owner's textured Meshy export supplied on 2026-10-20, named `Meshy_AI_Stonewatch_Beacon_1020143106_texture.glb`. Both copies are 7,833,208 bytes with SHA-256 `74406720a6aaeef3a465cfcebea08439735bb81c6694aedc56bdc638b194a4fe`.

The GLB is a static lighthouse mesh with 15,344 vertices, 11,739 triangles, UVs, normals, and one material. It embeds three 2048 × 2048 JPEG maps for base color, metallic/roughness, and normals. There is no skin, skeleton, or animation. Its roughly 0.95-unit height is centered on the origin.

The scene uses `lantern_tower_civic.glb`, a locally modified derivative built by Blender 5.0.1 with [`scripts/build_tower_variant.py`](../../../scripts/build_tower_variant.py). Its SHA-256 is `756f390986fa34889648545973c85701ab355d96b08b0338f4ef9a524ee07874`. From the source bottom through normalized Y `-0.22`, the script raises the door and stone plinth 18%; it compresses the long upper shaft to about 46.5% of its source height, retaining the lantern chamber's own height and the same `10.63 m` overall placed height. In a same-depth rendered comparison, the door leaf projects about 110 pixels high beside the clerk's 118 pixels, compared with about 91 pixels for the unstretched variant. The source GLB remains untouched. The derivative retains the same three embedded JPEG payloads byte for byte. [The tower slot](../../scripts/hub_environment.gd) scales it by `(13, 15, 11)` and offsets it by `(0, 7.13745, 0)`, placing the base on the floor and making the tower about `10.63 m` high and `5.0 × 5.0 m` across. The imported tower uses a scene-owned `5.2 × 10.7 × 5.2 m` column collider that keeps the close chase camera out of the shaft; the primitive fallback retains its original `4 × 2.5 × 4 m` collider. The beacon light moves to the derivative's lantern height. Review the scale, offset, footprint, and light position when substituting a model with different source dimensions.

Both tower `.glb.import` files keep their textures embedded in the imported scene and prevent duplicate extracted JPEG files. Godot 4.3 on macOS imported and displayed the shorter derivative in Lantern Quay; its visual proportions were compared in identical camera views near the clerk. The current release rights review covers these exports and their derivatives.

## Seaside Garden

`garden_optimized.glb` is the playable garden visual, derived from the project owner's `Meshy_AI_Seaside_Garden_Terrac_1021060957_texture.glb` export. The raw source remains untouched and outside the repository. It is 143,647,300 bytes, with SHA-256 `b6eafb660b88005088d96685c07f5f5ea509a7908ab0a66a52c76a86443f8d9e`, one fused mesh, 4,498,292 triangles, and one material containing three embedded 2048 × 2048 JPEG textures. It has no skeleton or animations.

[`tools/prepare_garden_mesh.sh`](../../../tools/prepare_garden_mesh.sh) checks that source hash and runs `gltfpack 1.3` from meshoptimizer tag `v1.3` (commit `9e1f07b159d3cb777f1c67ed31fc11fd117986f4`). It produces the one-mesh `garden_optimized.glb`: 39,040,004 bytes, 899,658 triangles, SHA-256 `14e0087e7e86782648dc120ba0e656233541ce0035e5bb2846972fd456a1e358`. The derivative retains three embedded 2048 × 2048 JPEG maps. It uses regular glTF buffers, without a runtime `EXT_meshopt_compression`, KTX2, or WebP dependency. The script documents the gltfpack build and command line. Godot imports the GLB as a `PackedScene` with its embedded maps, rather than shipping duplicate extracted JPEGs.

[`GardenLayout`](../../scripts/garden_layout.gd) supplies a shared placement for the visual, collision proxies and minimap. The garden is enlarged by `1.7` from its first import (uniform scale `15.64`, yaw `−85.5°`), fitting the actual bridge mouth to the four-metre plank approach at world X `32`, Z `−2`. The scene adds a stone sill over the irregular mesh cut edge and fits its approach beams to the imported posts. Coarse garden bounds are X `38..62`, Z `−14..6`; the map depicts the fitted terrace polygon inside those bounds. Scene-owned collision keeps the bridge level and follows the roughly `0.58 m` rise inside the gate; the dense mesh remains visual only. The original GLB and optimized derivative are unchanged by this placement correction. The generated missing-asset fallback is enlarged with the same fit. See the [district map and validation captures](../../../docs/hub-map.md). This mesh still needs a performance pass on target hardware.
