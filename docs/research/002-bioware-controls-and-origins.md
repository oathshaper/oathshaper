# BioWare controls and Dragon Age: Origins

> Comparative research and independent design proposals. See the [Research and third-party rights notice](NOTICE.md); references do not imply affiliation or permission to reuse source-game assets.

Research date: 2026-10-22. Focused verification; two original EA manuals.

## Research summary

The original Xbox 360 Mass Effect manual supports B to holster. Dragon Age: Origins fits the real-time-with-pause family with a distinct four-person party, conditional companion tactics and fantasy equipment presentation.

## Findings

1. **Mass Effect:** the original Xbox 360 manual lists X or left trigger for readying, right trigger for fire, and B to holster. Oathshaper adopts X/Q to draw, RT/LMB to strike and B/Esc to sheathe, while View opens its map. This comparison is edition-specific. [EA manual, printed pages 2 and 12](https://eaassets-a.akamaihd.net/eahelp/manuals/mass-effect-manuals_Microsoft%20XBOX360_en_US.pdf).
2. **Dragon Age: Origins:** its PC manual describes pausing, a party of four, warrior/mage/rogue roles, stamina and mana, and ordered condition/action companion tactics. These support Oathbound under the Command family; a separate initiative scheduler is unnecessary. [EA manual, printed pages 7, 9, 15–17 and 29–30](https://eaassets-a.akamaihd.net/eahelp/manuals/dragon-age-origins-manuals_PC.pdf).

Oathbound uses original names and a muted red accent. Its kit includes Ashguard sword, kite shield, worn mail, cinder staff, lockpick roll and groundleaf salve. Talents & Tactics previews Shield bash, Ashbolt, Mend and Mark target. Conditional tactics, class builds and resources belong to the later Rust model.

## Gaps and method

Queries: original Mass Effect Xbox 360 manual holster controls; Dragon Age: Origins official PC manual pause/party/tactics. Both PDFs were read from EA. No game art, characters, code or rules text was imported. Practice attacks are presentation-only; the manuals describe design references rather than implemented Oathshaper schedulers.
