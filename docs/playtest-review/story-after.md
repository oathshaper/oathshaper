## ivo_records_passage

## mara_crew
Mara: Six dockhands. They went across to mend the sea wall before the tide came in. The road is under water now. The garden walk is their only dry way home.

## mara_lamp
Mara: The pier marker shows where the boards end. Ivo can watch our crossing if it stays lit. It won't stop the machines, but it will keep us out of the channel.

## clerk_ownership
Clerk: Ivo won't lead an attack on someone's property on my word alone. The ledger records the public right of way. Take it to him, then recover the command seal so we can find out who ordered the closure.

## ivo_sentries
Ivo: They are harbor guards made of iron. Normally they keep people away from the flooded road. The man in red has fitted his own command seal. Disable the machines and take that seal from him. I'll help; Mara can heal us.

## premise
Mara's crew is stranded beyond the flooded road. The raised garden walk is their way home, but a man in a red coat has ordered the harbor's watch machines to block it.

## briefing
Ivo: "The ledger proves this is a public walk. Those two machines are keeping Mara's crew on the far side. The man in red carries their command seal."

Mara: "I can mend wounds. You and Ivo keep them away from me, and we can get my people home."

## objective
Disable the two watch machines and recover the raider's command seal.

## discovery
The raider's command seal bears a private mark. It ordered the harbor machines to bar a public walk.

## resolution
Clerk: "This is a private command seal. The ledger gives its owner no right to close the walk. I'll keep both as evidence."

Mara: "The machines are down. I can bring my crew home now. Thank you for coming with us."
